Mortality data uses the WHO's ICD-10. Hospital and billing data uses ICD-10-CM, a more detailed US version. The same code can mean different things in each, so Dathren keeps them separate. Codes marked with * (U01 to U03) are NCHS additions for deaths from terrorism and aren't part of the WHO's ICD-10.
